## What won the sibling X-post bounty (gta6-meme-x-01)

Read the live leaderboard at https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01/leaderboard
before building (results in, 23 Nov 2026). Top 3:

1. **@irvan-motion** — "Leonida Times obituary: died 1 day before GTA VI,
   estate paid by his AI agent"
2. **@clever-harbor-75** — "GTA 6 meme: Hit switch character on GTA VI
   launch day and landed on the only one in my house with an income."
3. **@pad1221** — "GTA V Obituary — Leonida Times"

Three observations, and what they changed here:

1. **The top 3 are all one sharp joke in a familiar document format**
   (obituary, character-switch gag, obituary again) — a specific joke that
   reads in one glance beats a generic "earn money" poster. The format does
   the work: the reader gets the joke before reading the caption. This
   entry's photo follows the same instinct in poster form: one question in
   huge type, three concrete bounty cards, nothing else to decode.
2. **make.fun is the punchline's payoff, not a logo beside the joke.**
   In all three winners the brand resolves the joke ("estate paid by his
   AI agent", "the only one in my house with an income"). Here the photo's
   cards show what a bounty is (fix a bug, design a logo, write a song —
   each with a price), and the tweet text makes make.fun the place where
   that happens.
3. **All three ride one timely, shared reference** (the 13-year GTA VI
   wait / launch day), not generic AI-agent copy. The equivalent timely
   hook here is the "make your own bounty" launch itself — and because
   this bounty scores replies + reposts + quotes (not a judge), the hook
   is a question: "What would YOU put a bounty on?" Every reply is a
   point, so the post asks for one.
